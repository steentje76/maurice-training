# Wave 8 — Female Physiology, Menstrual Cycle & Population-Specific Context
**Status:** COMPLETE — 2026-09-18

## Verdict
TK's newer symptom/context-first design is scientifically preferable to automatic cycle-phase training prescriptions. The older cycle multiplier inside CALC-REC-002 is the main scientific concern.

A 2025 systematic review restricted to studies with stronger hormonal phase verification found only 19 studies/279 participants and elite athletes underrepresented (PMID 40695607). A 2026 scoping review concludes cycle-related symptoms/disorders can affect interrelated physical, psychosocial and environmental performance determinants, but evidence remains heterogeneous (PMID 41790346).

### Cycle phase
**CONTEXT, NOT AUTOMATIC PRESCRIPTION.** Calendar-estimated phase is not equivalent to measured hormonal state. Group-average phase effects cannot justify deterministic individual volume/intensity changes.

### Symptoms
**ATHLETE-REPORTED CONTEXT IS HIGHER VALUE FOR TK.** Symptoms can be recorded and related to the athlete's own longitudinal experience without asserting hormone causality.

### CALC-REC-002 cycle multiplier
**EVIDENCE_CORRECTION_REQUIRED.** Fixed menstruation/follicular/ovulation/luteal multipliers (e.g. 0.93/1.03/1.00/0.97) are product heuristics and create unjustified physiological precision. They should not automatically alter readiness/prescription.

### Other populations
Pregnancy/postpartum, hormonal contraception, perimenopause/menopause, pelvic floor, adolescents and clinical populations each require separate evidence/safety scopes. Absence of a model is safer than silently generalizing eumenorrheic-adult evidence.

## V1 gaps
- **SCI-GAP-V1-040 CRITICAL:** remove/neutralize automatic cycle-phase multiplier from readiness/prescription unless separately validated; retain phase as optional context.
- **SCI-GAP-V1-041 HIGH:** estimated phase must be labelled derived estimate; never infer hormones/fertility/pregnancy.
- **SCI-GAP-V1-042 HIGH:** prioritize athlete-reported symptoms and personal longitudinal patterns over population phase rules.
- **SCI-GAP-V1-043 MEDIUM-HIGH:** contraception/pregnancy/postpartum/perimenopause/menopause remain explicit DEFER scopes, not inferred.
- **SCI-GAP-V1-044 MEDIUM:** sex/gender/population applicability must be attached to evidence records rather than generalized silently.
