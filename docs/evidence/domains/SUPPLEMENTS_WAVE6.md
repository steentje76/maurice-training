# Wave 6 — Supplements & Ergogenic Aids
**Status:** COMPLETE — 2026-09-18

## Verdict
A supplement library is scientifically appropriate only as an evidence/provenance knowledge layer. It must not become an autonomous prescribing engine. Evidence is substance-, outcome-, population-, dose- and protocol-specific.

### Creatine
**SUPPORTED for selected performance outcomes.** A 2025 meta-analysis of 69 studies/1937 participants found small significant improvements in several strength/power outcomes when creatine was combined with training (PMID 40944139), but not every outcome was significant. Avoid blanket “works for everyone” wording. Kidney/creatinine language must retain healthy-population and clinical-context boundaries.

### Caffeine
**SUPPORTED_WITH_CONTEXT.** Umbrella-review evidence supports ergogenic effects across several performance domains, with moderate overall evidence and variable prediction intervals (PMID 30926628). Response, habitual intake, sleep, side effects and timing matter. No universal “optimal” dose should be generated by AI.

### Dietary nitrate/beetroot
**MIXED/CONTEXT_DEPENDENT.** 2025 umbrella review of 20 meta-analyses found benefits for some outcomes and null findings for others, with many included reviews low/critically-low AMSTAR-2 quality (PMID 40085422). Outcome/population/protocol must be visible.

### Beta-alanine / sodium bicarbonate
**CONTEXT_DEPENDENT.** Effects are task/protocol specific; a 2024 combined-supplement meta-analysis did not find a clear overall ergogenic effect for either alone in that dataset (PMID 38952910). GI/tolerability context is relevant for bicarbonate.

### Other supplements
No supplement inherits evidence from its category. BCAA/EAA, citrulline, ashwagandha and future products each require claim-level review. Product-label data are not efficacy evidence.

## Safety/governance
- No disease treatment claims.
- No interaction/contraindication inference without an approved source/rule.
- No AI-generated dose.
- Custom user-added supplement gets **no accidental evidence inheritance**.
- Evidence freshness/version and source must be visible.
- Sport anti-doping/contamination risk is distinct from physiological efficacy and should be represented separately if implemented.

## V1 gaps
- **SCI-GAP-V1-029 HIGH:** every supplement claim must bind to substance+outcome+population+protocol evidence; no category inheritance.
- **SCI-GAP-V1-030 HIGH:** AI may explain registered claims but may not invent dose, safety or interaction advice.
- **SCI-GAP-V1-031 MEDIUM-HIGH:** efficacy, safety, legality/anti-doping and product-quality/contamination are separate dimensions.
- **SCI-GAP-V1-032 MEDIUM:** evidence freshness/supersession required for supplement claims.
- **SCI-GAP-V1-033 MEDIUM:** mixed/null outcomes must remain visible; “evidence-backed supplement” is too broad without outcome qualification.
